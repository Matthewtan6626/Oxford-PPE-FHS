**TIPS FOR KNOWLEDGE AND REALITY**

In general, I suggest studying 5 topics (not everything comes out). Some topics are very broad and require much more studying than others (for example, modality is one such topic that has many things to study for versus something like epistemic injustice or contextualism) but may be more your cup of tea. I studied 5 topics: contextualism (didn't come out), modality, epistemic injustice, persistence, metaphysics of race. I eventually ditched Analysis of Knowledge because I found that too difficult; the easy topics tend to be those that are more self contained (epistemic injustice, metaphysics of race) and also those with very tractable debate structures (persistence, modality). Though to caveat, modality is conceptually challenging, but has a tractable debate structure (clearly delineated schools of thought).
