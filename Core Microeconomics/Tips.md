**TIPS FOR CORE MICRO**

Unfortunately, you probably have to study almost everything for core micro, except for social choice theory and trade/open econ GE. Try your best to do Q10 (the math question), but it's not the end of the world if you don't (I did two essays for my core micro exam and it turned out fine). 

For Core Micro, they put a premium on explaining steps. For instance, writing out the definition of the Nash equilibrium before giving the answer to what the NE in the game is. Alternatively, you have to say "by Walras law, because n-1 markets clear, the n-th market clears". Do things slowly and methodically. 

For the essay, just churn out as many plans as possible. For many questions (such as IO questions), you want to have a "model-centric" essay that basically takes a particular model as the centrepiece and explores the extensions of these models and their corresponding consequences. Make sure you note down the assumptions of the model and highlight the economic intuition behind any result i.e. don't just state the result, highlight why the result is significant or makes economic sense.
