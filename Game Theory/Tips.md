**TIPS FOR GAME THEORY**

Paper is split into Part A (Core) and Part B (Applied) with 3 questions each and you should four questions. You must know everything in Core because that informs Applied.

Be sure that you know everything in Core, including stuff like mixing with continuous actions (came out in my year). Game Theory is a paper where studying content 
has very little marginal benefit as opposed to practicing questions. Just keep on practicing questions. 

Some Applied Topics can be quite hard. I suggest definitely doing Repeated Games and Evolution (though they don't always come out). Bargaining is also a common third choice (but 
in my experience can sometimes be quite hard). Reputation games are just PBE questions but more complicated and with a particular setting.
