**TIPS FOR PHIL LOGIC**

Practice the past year papers. Frankly, you can study almost all the topics save for one. I did everything except 2D modal logic (because no one does this). Sit down with some prima facie more difficult topics (such as second order logic) because there's high ROI in really toughing it out and learning how to do these questions (because the SOL questions are always the same). 

Make sure you know all the arguments for the essay because they can make the ceiling of your score higher (say from low 70s to high 70s). In particular, skim through arguments for vagueness (i.e. three valued logics) because it's not included in the course readings.
