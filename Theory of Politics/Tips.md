**TIPS FOR THEORY OF POLITICS**

Caveat: didn't take this paper for FHS only collections. But I suggest studying 5 topics that are linked to one another. I studied Democracy, Liberty, Civil Disobedience, Libertarianism and Equality. I also suggest having your own system of thought and taking a stand (for instance, I was partial to relational egalitarianism and it influenced all my arguments for all the topics i.e. I thought relational egalitarianism was the correct ideal for equality, which in turn made me think liberty is underwritten by equal participation, which in turn made me think that democracy is intrinsically valuable in being the only way to secure equal participation (republicanism). Civil disobedience is, therefore, trying to secure equal participation too. I also thought that libertarianism was underwritten by relational egalitarianism).
