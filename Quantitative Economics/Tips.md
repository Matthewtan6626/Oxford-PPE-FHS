**TIPS FOR QUANTITATIVE ECONOMICS**

Not really any tips -- just remember key derivations such as for the FWL, OVB etc. This allows you to do the math question (Q4). Try to get as much tutor feedback as possible for the open ended questions (i.e. how to answer questions that ask you to evaluate validity of 2SLS). It's quite irritating that there's no tractable formula to obtain the correct answer and I think how well you do in this paper really depends on tutor quality. 

You can satisfice by not studying co-integration/unit roots etc for time series, but I think one should and try to do the time series question in part B.
